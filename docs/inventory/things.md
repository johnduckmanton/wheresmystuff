# Managing Things

Things are the core of your inventory — every item you want to track. This page covers everything you can do with things.

## Adding a New Thing

1. Navigate to **Things** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Thing**.
3. Enter a **name** (required).
4. Fill in any optional fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Description | A brief note about the item |
| Make / Brand | Manufacturer or brand name |
| Model | Model name or number |
| Serial Number | Unique identifier for the item |
| Location | Where the item currently is |
| Room | Specific room within the location |
| Category | Type of item (e.g., Electronics, Furniture) |
| Owner | Person who owns the item |
| Tags | Keywords for searching and grouping |
| Date Purchased | When you bought it |
| Purchased From | Where you bought it |
| Purchase Price | How much you paid |
| Warranty Details | Warranty information |
| Notes | Any additional information |

5. Click **Save**.

## Editing Thing Details

1. On the **Things** page, click on the item you want to edit.
2. The item detail view opens, showing all current information.
3. Click **Edit** to modify any field.
4. Make your changes and click **Save**.

## Adding Photos

You can attach photos to any thing to help identify it or document its condition.

1. Open the thing you want to add a photo to.
2. In the photos section, click **Add Photo** or drag and drop an image.
3. The photo uploads to secure cloud storage and appears in the item's photo gallery.

!!! tip
    Photos are stored securely and are only accessible to users who have access to the inventory.

## AI Photo Recognition

The app includes AI-powered photo recognition that can help identify items and suggest descriptions.

1. When adding or editing a thing, look for the **AI Photo** option.
2. Take or upload a photo of the item.
3. The AI analyzes the image and suggests a description, category, or other details.
4. Review the suggestions and accept or modify them before saving.

!!! note
    AI recognition works best with clear, well-lit photos of individual items. Results are suggestions — always review before accepting.

## Tags and Search

Tags are keywords you attach to things to make them easier to find and group.

### Adding Tags

1. When adding or editing a thing, find the **Tags** field.
2. Type a tag name and press Enter to add it.
3. Add as many tags as you like.

### Searching by Tags

1. On the **Things** page, use the search and filter controls.
2. Select one or more tags to filter by.
3. Choose **Match all** (AND) to show items that have all selected tags, or **Match any** (OR) to show items that have at least one.

### Tag Suggestions

As you type a tag, the app suggests existing tags from your inventory. This helps keep your tags consistent and avoids duplicates.

## Barcode Scanning

You can scan barcodes to quickly look up or identify items.

1. On the **Things** page or when adding a new thing, look for the barcode scan option.
2. Point your device's camera at the barcode.
3. The app reads the barcode and can associate it with the item's metadata.

!!! note
    Barcode scanning requires camera access. On mobile devices, you may need to grant camera permissions when prompted.

## Viewing Thing Details

The thing detail view shows all information about an item at a glance:

- **Name and description** at the top
- **Photos** in a gallery view
- **Location and room** where the item is
- **Category** the item belongs to
- **Owner** who the item belongs to
- **Tags** attached to the item
- **Purchase details** — date, source, price
- **Warranty information**
- **Date added** to the inventory
- **Container** — if the item is currently packed in a container
- **Notes** — any additional information

If the item is packed in a container, you'll see a link to that container's details.
