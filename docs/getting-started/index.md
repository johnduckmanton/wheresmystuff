# Getting Started

This guide walks you through creating an account, signing in, and getting oriented in the app.

## What the App Does

Where's My Stuff! helps you:

- Keep a detailed inventory of everything you own
- Organize items by location, room, and category
- Track who owns what
- Pack items into containers for a move
- Generate QR codes for containers so you can scan and see what's inside
- Plan and manage moving projects with tasks and budgets

All your data is stored securely in the cloud, so you can access it from any device.

## Signing Up

1. Open the app and you'll see the sign-in page.
2. Click the **Sign In** link at the bottom, or if you don't have an account, click **Create Account**.
3. Enter your **email address** and choose a **password**.

!!! info "Password requirements"
    Your password must be at least 8 characters and include:

    - At least one uppercase letter
    - At least one lowercase letter
    - At least one number

4. Confirm your password by typing it again.
5. Click **Create Account**.
6. Check your email for a **verification code**. Enter the code on the verification screen.
7. Once verified, you'll be signed in automatically.

## Signing In

1. Open the app to reach the sign-in page.
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, you'll be prompted to enter a code from your authenticator app.

## Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your password:

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your email for a reset code.
4. Enter the code and choose a new password.

## Two-Factor Authentication (MFA)

You can add an extra layer of security by enabling two-factor authentication. This requires an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password) on your phone.

For setup instructions, see [Account Management — Two-Factor Authentication](../account/index.md#two-factor-authentication-mfa).

## Choosing an Inventory

After signing in, you'll land on the **Home** page. If you belong to multiple inventories (for example, one you created and one someone shared with you), use the **inventory selector** in the sidebar to switch between them.

The inventory selector appears at the top of the sidebar navigation. Select the inventory you want to work with, and all pages will show data for that inventory.

!!! tip
    If this is your first time using the app, head to [First Steps](first-steps.md) to create your first inventory and start adding items.
